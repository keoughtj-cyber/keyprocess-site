FAQ

Common questions

Can work order follow-ups really be automated?

Yes. Every vendor with a work order open past a set number of days gets a personal status request on schedule, and the replies come straight back to your maintenance team.

Will vendors know it is automated?

Each message is personal to the vendor and the work order, written the way your team would write it, and replies go to a real person.

Does this work with our maintenance software?

Maintenance automations are built around the software and email you already use, so there is nothing new for your team or your vendors to learn.

How much time does it save?

One property management office went from typing 100 or more follow-ups by hand to zero, saving about eight hours a month while following up on every stalled work order.

FAQ

Common questions

What can an online store automate?

Promotions and product launches, order and revenue tracking, product reviews, customer follow-ups, low-stock alerts, and weekly sales reports. If your team does it the same way every week, it can usually run on its own.

Does this work with our store platform?

Yes. Automations are built around the store, email, and software you already use, so there is nothing new for your team to learn.

Is this only for big stores?

No. Whether your team is 3 people or 300, automation takes repetitive work off everyone's plate, and the more orders and products you have, the bigger the savings.

How much does e-commerce automation cost?

A single automated process starts at $1,000, a package of 3 to 5 connected automations starts at $3,000, and Automation Care starts at $500 a month. Every project starts with a free audit and a fixed quote. Our automation pricing guide explains what drives the cost.

FAQ

Common questions

What can a home services business automate?

Quote follow-ups, appointment reminders, job status updates, review requests, invoice reminders, permit and license renewals, and weekly job and revenue reports. If your office does it the same way for every job, it can usually run on its own.

Does this work with the software we already use?

Yes. Automations are built around the scheduling, invoicing, email, and text tools you already use, so there is nothing new for your crews or office to learn.

Is this only for big contractors?

No. Whether your team is 3 people or 300, automation takes repetitive office work off everyone's plate. Smaller shops often feel it most, because the owner is usually the one doing the paperwork at night.

Will my customers know it's automated?

Messages go out in your name and your words, at the right moment for each job. Customers just notice that you follow up faster and never forget.

How much does home services automation cost?

A single automated process starts at $1,000, a package of 3 to 5 connected automations starts at $3,000, and Automation Care starts at $500 a month. Every project starts with a free audit and a fixed quote. Our automation pricing guide explains what drives the cost.

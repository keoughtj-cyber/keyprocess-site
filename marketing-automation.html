FAQ

Common questions

What rental marketing can be automated?

Posting new listings, updating price drops and rented units, publishing social media, announcing listings by email, measuring which listing sites bring tours, and finding new rental owners to market to.

Do we still approve what gets posted?

Yes. You decide what needs approval, and automations handle everything else, such as resizing, captions, and posting to each platform.

Can it write our captions?

Yes. Captions, titles, and hashtags are drafted for each platform, so one approved post becomes five without any rewriting.

Can it help us win new management business?

Yes. One automation finds rental owners in target ZIP codes every month and prepares a postcard campaign for one-click approval.

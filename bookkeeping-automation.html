FAQ

Common questions

What bookkeeping tasks can a property manager automate?

Utility bill tracking, invoice logging, exception alerts, late payment reminders, owner reports, and month-end checklists are all strong candidates, because they follow the same rules every time.

Is our financial data safe?

Automations are set up in accounts your company owns and controls, and they only touch the information each task needs.

Will this replace our bookkeeper?

No. It removes the data entry and checking, so your bookkeeper spends time on the exceptions and decisions instead of typing.

How much time does it save?

Daily water bill tracking alone saved one office about eight hours of bookkeeping every month.

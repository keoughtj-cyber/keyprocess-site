25 property management tasks you can automate

If a task repeats, follows rules, or lives in email and spreadsheets, it can probably run on its own. Here are 25 of the most common ones we see in property management offices, grouped by department.

Leasing

  • 1. Approval letters: filled in from the application, with no retyping.
  • 2. Lease preparation: the right tenant, unit, rent, and dates, ready for review.
  • 3. Move-in payment summaries: deposits, prorated rent, and fees totaled the same way every time.
  • 4. Renewal reminders: expirations caught early so renewals never become a scramble.
  • 5. Daily leasing reports: one end-of-day summary of approvals and applications.

More on leasing automation →

Maintenance

  • 6. Stalled work order follow-ups: every vendor with an aging work order asked for an update each week.
  • 7. Vendor reply routing: updates delivered to the right coordinator automatically.
  • 8. Resident repair updates: status messages sent at the right time, without a phone call.
  • 9. Recurring maintenance schedules: inspections, filters, and seasonal work never missed.
  • 10. Vendor document alerts: expiring insurance and licenses flagged before the next job.

More on maintenance automation →

Bookkeeping

  • 11. Utility bill tracking: payments matched to each property and logged daily.
  • 12. Exception alerts: unusual amounts, missing bills, and duplicates flagged for review.
  • 13. Invoice logging: vendor invoices recorded without data entry.
  • 14. Late payment reminders: friendly, consistent reminders on schedule.
  • 15. Owner reports: statements and portfolio numbers delivered on the same day every month.

More on bookkeeping automation →

Marketing

  • 16. Listing updates: new rentals added, price drops updated, and rented units removed nightly.
  • 17. Social media posting: one upload published to five platforms with tailored captions.
  • 18. Listing announcements: new listings, price drops, and sales promoted in one click.
  • 19. Lead source reporting: every showing matched to the site that produced it.
  • 20. Owner prospecting: rental owners in target ZIP codes found every month.

More on marketing automation →

Compliance and operations

  • 21. Rental license checks: every property's license status checked on schedule, with alerts on problems.
  • 22. Inspection deadlines: reminders well before certificates and inspections expire.
  • 23. Team calendar posts: birthdays, anniversaries, and holiday closures posted all year.
  • 24. Request forms and approvals: requests collected, routed to the right person, and tracked.
  • 25. Department to-do lists: each team starts the day with a list of exactly what needs them.

FAQ

Common questions

What is the easiest property management task to automate first?

Start with something your team does every week that follows the same steps each time, such as work order follow-ups, utility bill tracking, or listing updates. These are quick to build and save time from the first day.

How do I know which tasks are worth automating?

Multiply the hours a task takes each week by what those hours cost you. The tasks with the biggest number, and the clearest rules, usually pay for themselves fastest. A free automation audit will rank them for you.

Do I need to switch property management software to automate?

No. Automations can be built around the software and email you already use.

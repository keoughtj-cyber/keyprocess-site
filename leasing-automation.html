FAQ

Common questions

What parts of leasing can be automated?

Anything that repeats the same steps for every applicant or tenant: approval letters, lease preparation, move-in payment summaries, renewal reminders, follow-ups for missing documents, and daily or weekly leasing reports.

Does my team still review leases?

Yes. The paperwork is prepared automatically, and your team reviews and approves it. Nothing goes out that your team hasn't seen.

Do we need new leasing software?

No. Leasing automations are built around the software and email you already use, so nobody has to learn a new system.

How much time does leasing automation save?

In one property management office, lease approvals dropped from about two hours to ten minutes each, which adds up to roughly 55 workdays a year.

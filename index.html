FAQ

Common questions

What does a business automation service do?

We take the repetitive work your team does every day, such as filling in paperwork, chasing follow-ups, logging bills, posting to social media, and building reports, and make it run on its own. Your team just reviews and approves.

Do we have to switch software?

No. Automations are built around the software and email you already use, so nobody has to learn a new system.

How fast will we see results?

Most single-process projects go live within a few weeks, and the time savings start the first day they run.

What happens if something breaks?

With an Automation Care plan, we monitor everything, fix issues quickly, and keep improving your automations as your business grows.

What industries do you work with?

Any business with repetitive work. Our case studies come from property management and e-commerce, but the same patterns (approvals, follow-ups, bookkeeping, marketing, and reporting) show up in home services, professional offices, healthcare practices, agencies, retail, nonprofits, and more.

Do you work with businesses anywhere?

Yes. We work remotely with businesses anywhere in the U.S., and everything is set up and supported online.

Can you automate something that isn't listed here?

Yes. The case studies are a sample of dozens of automations we have built. If a task is repetitive, follows rules, or lives in email and spreadsheets, there is a good chance it can be automated. The free audit is where we figure that out together.

Is there a guarantee?

Yes. If your automation doesn't save your team time in the first 30 days, we fix it free until it does. Any build also comes with your first month of Automation Care free.

What size company is this for?

Solo owners and small teams up to larger companies. Whether your team is 3 people or 300, automations grow with you without adding staff, and the more repetitive work you have, the bigger the savings.

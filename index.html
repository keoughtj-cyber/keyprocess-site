Compliance: rental licenses checked and certificates pulled every month

When Philadelphia's Safe and Healthy Homes rules tightened enforcement on landlords, keeping rental licensing current stopped being optional. Every rental needs a valid license and a current Certificate of Rental Suitability for each new tenant, and looking them up one property at a time on the city's site took hours. Now, every month, each property is looked up automatically, its rental license is found, and the certificate is requested and saved, with the certificate number, issue date, and expiration date recorded. If a license can't be found or a certificate fails, the team is told right away so they can fix it before it delays a move-in.

Monthlylicense and certificate check for every property
0 lookupsdone by hand on the city's website
Instantalerts when a license or certificate needs attention

Social calendar: birthdays, work anniversaries, and holidays posted all year

Celebrating the team and announcing holiday hours is good for culture and good for the brand, but it is easy to forget when everyone is busy. Now it is set up once at the start of the year: each team member's birthday, work anniversary, role, and photo, plus every holiday, its date, and the office hours or closure message. From then on, each celebration and holiday posts to social media on the right morning with its photo and caption, without anyone touching it. Two business days before each birthday or anniversary, marketing and leadership get a heads-up so they can plan something, and once the post goes live, the whole team gets a note to go like and share it.

Showing sources: know where every tour comes from, and which ones turn into leases

Listings go out to a dozen rental sites and social channels, but when a prospect books a tour, nobody really knows which ad brought them in, so marketing money gets spent on guesses. Now, every Monday morning, the past week's scheduled showings are matched to the most likely source behind each one, with a confidence rating, and lined up against what actually happened with the prospect. The report shows where showings are coming from, which sources are turning into applications and leases, which ones lead to the most cancellations and no-shows, and how the overall funnel performed, so the team can put its budget behind the sites that work.

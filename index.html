FAQ

Common questions

What does a property management automation service do?

We take the repetitive work your team does every day, such as drafting leases, chasing vendors, logging bills, posting listings, and checking licenses, and make it run on its own. Your team just reviews and approves.

Do we have to switch software?

No. Automations are built around the software and email you already use, so nobody has to learn a new system.

How fast will we see results?

Most single-process projects go live within a few weeks, and the time savings start the first day they run.

What happens if something breaks?

With an Automation Care plan, we monitor everything, fix issues quickly, and keep improving your automations as your portfolio grows.

Do you only work with Philadelphia property managers?

We specialize in the Philadelphia area, including Safe and Healthy Homes licensing compliance, and work remotely with property management companies anywhere in the U.S.

What size company is this for?

Property management companies managing roughly 50 to 1,000 units get the most out of it, because that is where manual work starts to outgrow the team.
